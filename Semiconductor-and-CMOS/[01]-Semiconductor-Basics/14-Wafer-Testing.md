# Wafer Testing

## 📖 What is Wafer Testing?

**Wafer Testing** is the process of checking whether the integrated circuits (ICs) on a **silicon wafer** are working correctly **before the wafer is cut into individual chips**.

It helps identify defective chips early, saving time and manufacturing cost.

---

## Simple Definition

> **Wafer Testing is the process of testing each chip on a silicon wafer to ensure it works correctly before packaging.**

---

## Why is Wafer Testing Important?

- Finds defective chips.
- Improves chip quality.
- Reduces manufacturing cost.
- Increases production yield.
- Ensures only working chips are packaged.

---

## How Does Wafer Testing Work?

1. The completed silicon wafer is placed on a testing machine.
2. Tiny **probe needles** touch the metal test pads on each chip.
3. Electrical signals are applied to the chip.
4. The chip's output is measured.
5. Good chips are marked for packaging, and defective chips are rejected.

---

## Wafer Testing Flow

```text
Completed Wafer
       ↓
Probe Testing
       ↓
Electrical Measurements
       ↓
Pass or Fail
       ↓
Good Chips → Packaging
Bad Chips → Rejected
```

---

## Advantages

- Detects faulty chips early.
- Improves product reliability.
- Saves packaging costs.
- Increases manufacturing efficiency.

---

## Applications

Wafer testing is used for:

- CPUs
- GPUs
- RAM
- ROM
- ASICs
- FPGAs
- Microcontrollers
