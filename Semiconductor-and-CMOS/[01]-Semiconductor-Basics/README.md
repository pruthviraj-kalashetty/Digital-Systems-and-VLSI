# 01. Semiconductor Basics

[![Stage](https://img.shields.io/badge/Stage-C--CMOS--Fundamentals-blue.svg)](#)
[![Focus](https://img.shields.io/badge/Focus-Semiconductor%20Technology-green.svg)](#)

This module introduces the fundamental concepts of semiconductor materials and the complete semiconductor manufacturing process used to build modern integrated circuits. It covers semiconductor physics, doping, N-type and P-type materials, silicon wafer fabrication, cleanroom technology, photolithography, wafer testing, chip packaging, and the semiconductor ecosystem.

These concepts form the foundation for understanding CMOS technology, MOSFETs, VLSI design, and modern semiconductor manufacturing.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By working through this module, you will be able to:

- Understand the fundamentals of semiconductor materials.
- Differentiate between intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors.
- Explain semiconductor doping and charge carrier formation.
- Understand N-type and P-type semiconductor materials.
- Learn the complete semiconductor manufacturing process from raw silicon to packaged ICs.
- Understand wafer fabrication, lithography, testing, and packaging.
- Gain an overview of the global semiconductor ecosystem.

## 🛠️ Core Concepts Covered

### 1. Semiconductor Materials

Understand the electrical properties of semiconductor materials and how intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors form the basis of modern electronic devices.

### 2. Doping Technology

Learn how controlled impurity doping creates N-type and P-type semiconductors, enabling the operation of diodes, transistors, and integrated circuits.

### 3. Silicon Wafer Manufacturing

Study the journey from raw silica to high-purity silicon wafers, including crystal growth, wafer slicing, polishing, and preparation for fabrication.

### 4. Semiconductor Fabrication

Understand how integrated circuits are manufactured using cleanroom environments, photolithography, etching, deposition, ion implantation, and advanced EUV lithography.

### 5. Testing and Packaging

Learn how fabricated wafers undergo electrical testing, quality assurance, dicing, packaging, and final inspection before deployment in electronic products.

### 6. Semiconductor Ecosystem

Explore the complete semiconductor value chain, including chip design companies, foundries, OSAT providers, EDA companies, equipment manufacturers, and end-product integration.
