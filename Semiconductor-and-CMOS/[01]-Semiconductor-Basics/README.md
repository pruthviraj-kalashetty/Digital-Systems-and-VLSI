# 01. Semiconductor Basics

[![Stage](https://img.shields.io/badge/Stage-Semiconductor--Basics--and--CMOS-blue.svg)](#)
[![Focus](https://img.shields.io/badge/Focus-Semiconductor%20Basics-green.svg)](#)

This module introduces the fundamental concepts of semiconductor materials that form the foundation of modern electronics and VLSI technology. It covers semiconductor classification, intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, doping techniques, and the characteristics of N-type and P-type semiconductors.

Understanding these concepts is essential before studying PN junctions, MOSFETs, CMOS technology, and transistor-level circuit design.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By working through this module, you will be able to:

- Understand the fundamental properties of semiconductor materials.
- Differentiate between conductors, semiconductors, and insulators.
- Explain intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors.
- Understand the process and importance of semiconductor doping.
- Differentiate between N-type and P-type semiconductors.
- Build a strong foundation for semiconductor devices and CMOS technology.

## 🛠️ Core Concepts Covered

### 1. Semiconductor Materials

Understand the electrical properties of conductors, semiconductors, and insulators, and learn why semiconductors are the foundation of modern electronic devices.

### 2. Intrinsic and Extrinsic Semiconductors

Study pure semiconductor materials and learn how controlled impurity addition modifies their electrical characteristics to meet practical device requirements.

### 3. Semiconductor Doping

Understand the process of introducing donor and acceptor impurities into semiconductor materials to increase conductivity and control charge carrier concentration.

### 4. N-Type and P-Type Semiconductors

Learn the formation, charge carriers, energy band characteristics, and electrical behavior of N-type and P-type semiconductors, which are the building blocks of semiconductor devices.

### 5. Foundation for CMOS Technology

Understand how semiconductor materials and doping techniques enable the fabrication of PN junctions, MOSFETs, CMOS logic circuits, and modern VLSI integrated circuits.
