# EUV Lithography (Extreme Ultraviolet Lithography)

## 📖 What is EUV Lithography?

**EUV (Extreme Ultraviolet) Lithography** is an advanced photolithography technique that uses **extreme ultraviolet (EUV) light** to print **extremely small circuit patterns** on a silicon wafer.

It is used to manufacture the latest and most powerful semiconductor chips.

---

## Simple Definition

> **EUV Lithography is an advanced chip manufacturing process that uses extreme ultraviolet light to create very tiny transistor patterns on a silicon wafer.**

---

## Why is EUV Lithography Important?

- Creates much smaller transistors.
- Increases chip performance.
- Reduces power consumption.
- Allows billions of MOSFETs to fit on a single chip.
- Used for advanced semiconductor technology.

---

## How Does EUV Lithography Work?

1. A silicon wafer is coated with **photoresist**.
2. An **EUV light source** generates extremely short-wavelength light.
3. The light is reflected by special mirrors through a **mask**.
4. The circuit pattern is transferred onto the wafer.
5. The wafer is developed and processed to create tiny transistor structures.

---

## Why is EUV Better than Traditional UV Lithography?

| Feature | UV Lithography | EUV Lithography |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|
| Light Used | Ultraviolet (UV) | Extreme Ultraviolet (EUV) |
| Pattern Size | Small | Much Smaller |
| Chip Density | Lower | Higher |
| Performance | Good | Better |
| Power Consumption | Higher | Lower |
