# Photolithography

## 📖 What is Photolithography?

**Photolithography** is the process of transferring a circuit pattern from a **mask** onto a **silicon wafer** using **ultraviolet (UV) light**.

It is one of the most important steps in semiconductor manufacturing because it creates the tiny patterns used to build MOSFETs and integrated circuits.

---

## Simple Definition

> **Photolithography is the process of using UV light and a mask to create circuit patterns on a silicon wafer.**

---

## Steps in Photolithography

1. Coat the silicon wafer with **photoresist** (a light-sensitive material).
2. Place a **mask** containing the circuit pattern over the wafer.
3. Shine **UV light** through the mask.
4. Develop the photoresist to reveal the required pattern.

---

## Why is Photolithography Important?

- Creates tiny transistor patterns.
- Enables fabrication of millions or billions of MOSFETs.
- Provides high precision for modern semiconductor chips.

---

## Applications

- CPUs
- GPUs
- Memory Chips
- ASICs
- FPGAs
- Microcontrollers
