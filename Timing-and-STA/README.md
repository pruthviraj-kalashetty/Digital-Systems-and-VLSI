## 👨‍💻 Executive Summary

I am an **Electronics & Communication Engineering undergraduate** at **LAEC, Bidar**, specializing in **ASIC Front-End RTL Design, Computer Architecture, and Digital VLSI Engineering**.

I am systematically developing an open-source, industry-aligned engineering portfolio tracking the complete digital design lifecycle—from combinational/sequential logic theory and synthesizable Verilog primitives to dual-clock asynchronous FIFOs, RISC-V SoC integration, and ASIC logic synthesis.

* 🎓 **Education:** B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering (5th Semester)
* 🎯 **Career Target:** ASIC RTL Design / Digital Front-End Design Engineer (2026/2027)
* 🌱 **Current Focus:** Synthesizable FSM Architectures, Clock Domain Crossing (CDC), Async FIFO Design, and Setup/Hold Timing Analysis
